# EARTHFLIGHT

## What?
This is a gamepad-controlled Apple Vision Pro viewer for Google Earth tiles in 3D. You fly around and look at things. The goal is to be comically simple - fire it up, use an (already-connected) gamepad. No settings screens or configuration options, tweaking means changing the code, and I have tweaked it to my personal preference.

## Why?
This project is here because there was not a simple Google Earth style viewer controlled by a gamepad, and a gamepad is a tried and trusted way to control flight. There are other great projects that do similar map viewing with gestures, but I couldn't get along with that control method.

## How?
These instructions assume a Nintendo Switch Pro controller, but will work for Xbox/Playstation even though the buttons are named differently.
 - Use the left thumbstick to go forwards/backwards/strafe.
 - Point in a new direction with the right thumbstick.
 - Shoulder triggers, top and bottom, will raise/lower altitude
 - Clicking the right thumbstick resets your horizon
 - Pressing up and down on the left d-pad will change the size of everything (ever wanted to be a giant?)
 - Pressing the + button on the right will let you speak, and teleport to where it things you said
 - Pressing the - button on the left will cycle the HUD view
 - Pressing the Home button will exit.
